About us

An independent IT consultancy in Tring

XD Technology Consulting is the trading name of XD TECHNOLOGY CONSULTING LTD, an information technology consultancy based in Tring, Hertfordshire. The company was incorporated in England and Wales on 12 May 2025 under company number 16443979.

We help organisations make clear, practical decisions about technology and then carry them through. That can mean assessing the systems you have today, shaping a roadmap, advising on software, cloud and infrastructure choices, supporting a project while it is under way, or giving an independent view on a system, supplier or programme.

As an independent, owner-managed consultancy, we work directly with you, without a sales layer in between, and give advice in plain language.

Services

How we can help

Four areas of IT consultancy, which can be used on their own or together as a project moves from idea to delivery.

Technology Strategy

We look at your current systems and how they support the business, then set out a practical technology roadmap aligned with your goals.

  • Review of current systems, processes and pain points
  • Priorities agreed against business objectives
  • A phased, realistic technology roadmap

Solution & Architecture Consulting

Advice on the design and selection of software, cloud and infrastructure solutions, so the options are understood before commitments are made.

  • Requirements and options clearly laid out
  • Solution design and architecture guidance
  • Independent input on software and supplier selection

Delivery Support

Hands-on support to plan, steer and deliver technology projects and transformation work, from early planning through to go-live.

  • Project planning and scoping
  • Ongoing steering, risk and issue tracking
  • Coordination between teams and suppliers

Reviews & Audits

Independent reviews of systems, suppliers and projects, finishing with clear, actionable recommendations you can act on.

  • Health checks of systems and projects
  • Supplier and contract arrangement reviews
  • Written findings with prioritised next steps
